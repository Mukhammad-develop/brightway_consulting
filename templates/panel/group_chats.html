How it works

  • Add the Telegram group/supergroup ID (negative number for supergroups, e.g. -1001234567890).
  • When a member asks about our services the bot replies with a DM invitation.
  • That user is put in a cooldown — the bot won't respond to them again until it expires.
  • If the user replies to the bot's message the cooldown is cancelled immediately.
  • If someone else replies to the bot's message the bot gives a short answer and redirects to DM.
  • The Behavior Prompt defines what topics the AI handles in this specific group.
