How Subjects work in the simplified bot flow
  1. Step 1 — Greeting: Bot greets the user and shows the list of active Subjects as buttons.
  2. Step 2 — Subject selection: User picks a Subject (or types it). Bot shows the services belonging to that Subject.
  3. Step 3 — Service selection: User picks a specific Service. Bot shows the Items to Collect list from the service configuration.
  4. Step 4 — Collection: Bot waits while user sends text, photos, and documents. When user types Done, the case is finalized.
  5. Step 5 — Finalization: Bot assigns the case to a consultant, sends a thank-you, and stops AI responses.

To configure the Items to Collect for each service, edit the service and fill in the Items to Collect field under AI Configuration.
